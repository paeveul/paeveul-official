Cookie Notice

Last updated: 30 May 2026

This Cookie Notice applies to Paeveul Web Services. For information on who is responsible for your personal data and how to contact us, see our Privacy Notice.

This Cookie Notice explains how Paeveul Web Services uses cookies on our website (paeveul.com).

What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device when you visit. Cookies help websites function properly and help site owners understand how visitors use their site.

Cookies Currently in Use

We use a minimal number of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any cookies that identify you personally.

Cookie name Set by What it does Type Duration
_GRECAPTCHA Google reCAPTCHA Protects our contact and homepage forms from spam and automated bots Functional Session / up to 6 months

Why we need the reCAPTCHA cookie: Our contact forms use Google reCAPTCHA v2 to verify that submissions come from real people, not bots. This cookie is required for the forms to work. It does not track you across websites and is not used for advertising purposes.

Google Fonts — Third-Party Data Access

Our website loads fonts from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) on every page. This is not a cookie in the traditional sense — it is a third-party network request. Each time you load a page, your IP address and browser information are sent to Google's servers so the fonts can be delivered. Google may also set short-lived caching cookies via fonts.gstatic.com as part of standard browser caching behaviour.

This is a technical characteristic of how web fonts are served from an external CDN. It is not advertising or tracking. For more information, refer to Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies We Do Not Currently Use

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel, or any other analytics or advertising cookies.

Cookies We May Add in the Future

We may add analytics or advertising tools in the future. If and when that happens, this Cookie Notice will be updated before those tools go live. If advertising or tracking cookies are introduced, a cookie consent mechanism will be added to the site so you can make an informed choice.

Tool Purpose if added Cookie type
Google Analytics 4 Website traffic analytics (pages visited, session duration, referral source) Analytics
Microsoft Clarity Session recording and heatmap analysis for UX improvement Analytics
Meta Pixel Tracks conversions and enables retargeting on Facebook and Instagram Advertising

How to Manage Cookies

You have full control over cookies. Here is how to manage them:

Option 1 — Browser settings

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Note: disabling the _GRECAPTCHA cookie may prevent our contact forms from working correctly, as reCAPTCHA is required for spam protection.

Option 2 — Google's privacy controls

You can manage how Google uses your data at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Changes to This Notice

We will update this page if our cookie use changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. If advertising cookies are ever deployed, we will introduce a cookie consent banner and update this notice accordingly.
