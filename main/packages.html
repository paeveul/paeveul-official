Pricing FAQ

Pricing questions,
answered straight.

For everything else (timelines, content, SEO, tech) see the full FAQ →

Why don't you publish prices? +

Templated prices lead to templated work. A 5-page site for a clinic isn't the same as a 5-page site for a B2B SaaS — pricing should reflect the actual project, not a sticker.

Are quotes flat-rate or hourly? +

Flat-rate by default. You see the total before we start, and that's what you pay. Hourly retainers are a separate add-on for ongoing work.

What's included in the quote? +

Design, build, deployment, on-page SEO basics, a launch checklist, and two weeks of post-launch tweaks. Platform fees (hosting, domain) are passed through at cost.

Can I switch tiers mid-project? +

Yes. If your needs grow during the build, we re-scope transparently. No surprise invoices, ever.
