05 FAQ

Things SME owners
usually ask first.

Quick answers. If you don't see yours, just enquire — we reply within 1–3 business working days.

How long does a website take to build?

Most Essential builds go live in 2–3 weeks. Professional builds typically run 4–6 weeks. Business projects scope to timeline before we start.

Do I need to prepare the content myself?

You bring the substance — brand info, services, photos if available. We help shape and structure it, and can provide copy guidance throughout.

Can you help with SEO?

Yes. Every build ships with technical SEO basics: structure, speed, schema. Professional and Business tiers include on-page optimisation and content scaffolding.

Are prices fixed?

No public prices because every project differs. After a short enquiry, we send a clear quote based on scope, pages, and timeline.

Can I request changes during the build?

Yes. Every package includes review rounds before launch. Scope changes are discussed transparently — no surprise invoices.

Do you offer monthly support?

Optional monthly retainers cover updates, monitoring, content edits, and incremental improvements. Available on Professional and Business.

Can payments be added later?

Yes. We architect builds so e-commerce, bookings, or payment flows can be added without a rebuild.
