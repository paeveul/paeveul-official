I
Process & Timeline
How long does a website take to build?

Most Essential builds go live in 2–3 weeks. Professional builds typically run 4–6 weeks. Business projects scope to timeline before we start — usually 6–10 weeks depending on integrations.

What does the build process look like?

Five steps: enquire, choose a package, build, review, launch. You get weekly check-ins, a working preview link from week one, and review rounds before anything ships.

Can I request changes during the build?

Yes. Every package includes review rounds before launch. Scope changes are discussed transparently — no surprise invoices, no hidden hour-tracking.

What if I want changes after launch?

Small post-launch tweaks are included for two weeks after ship. Beyond that, hourly retainers or monthly support kick in — whatever fits your pace.

II
Pricing & Packages
Are prices fixed?

No public prices because every project differs. After a short enquiry, we send a clear quote based on scope, pages, and timeline. Quotes are flat-rate by default, not hourly.

Why don't you publish prices?

Templated prices lead to templated work. A 5-page site for a clinic isn't the same as a 5-page site for a B2B SaaS — pricing should reflect the actual project, not a sticker.

What's included in the quote?

Design, build, deployment, on-page SEO basics, a launch checklist, and two weeks of post-launch tweaks. Any platform fees (hosting, domain) are passed through at cost.

Do you offer monthly support?

Optional monthly retainers cover updates, monitoring, content edits, and incremental improvements. Available on Professional and Business tiers, scoped to what you actually need.

III
Content & Design
Do I need to prepare the content myself?

You bring the substance — brand info, services, photos if available. We help shape and structure it, and can provide copy guidance throughout. Copy-only support is also available as an add-on.

Can you help with branding?

Light brand work (typography, colour, voice) is included on Professional and Business. Full brand identity is a separate engagement — we can recommend partners if needed.

Will the site work on mobile?

Every site we ship is responsive and mobile-first. We design for thumb-flow first, then scale up to desktop — not the other way around.

How many revision rounds are included?

Two structured review rounds, plus an unlimited number of small adjustments during the build. We aim for fewer, better-considered rounds rather than endless ping-pong.

IV
SEO & Performance
Can you help with SEO?

Yes. Every build ships with technical SEO basics: structure, speed, schema. Professional and Business tiers include on-page optimisation, content scaffolding, and a 30-day audit after launch.

Will my site rank on Google?

Honest answer: SEO is a long game. We build sites that are technically excellent and indexable, but rankings depend on content, competition, and time. We won't promise rankings; we will promise the right foundation.

What about Core Web Vitals?

Every site ships green on Core Web Vitals — that's the studio standard. If we can't hit it, we don't launch it.

V
Tech & Future
Can payments be added later?

Yes. We architect builds so e-commerce, bookings, or payment flows can be added without a rebuild. The foundation is payments-ready from day one on Professional and Business tiers.

Do I own the code?

Yes, fully. Code, design files, content — all yours. No vendor lock-in, no platform tax, no licensing trap.

What CMS do you use?

It depends on the project. We default to lightweight, no-lock-in options (Sanity, Payload, or static MD). Bigger projects get headless setups; smaller ones stay simple.

Do you build on WordPress?

Not typically. WordPress is great for some teams, but our defaults favour faster, less-bloated stacks. If you're committed to WP, we can recommend partners who specialise.
